How it works

From hello to a first visit, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Tell us what a good day looks like.

    Big, tappable chips instead of forms: music, cooking, casual walking, group meets, getting snacks. A senior (or their daughter, sitting next to them) can finish it in a couple of minutes.

  2. 02

    Have a real conversation with Sonia.

    One button, then just talk. Sonia asks about routines, mobility, the language they're most at home in, and what they miss doing. That conversation tells us far more than a checkbox ever could.

  3. 03

    Meet a companion who actually fits.

    We surface a short list of nearby companions with the languages, experience, and interests that line up. Then you request a walk, a visit, or help with an errand, and they show up in person.

Meet Sonia

We made the first step a conversation, not a form.

Onboarding is where most apps lose older users: tiny text fields, passwords, ten screens of dropdowns. So we replaced it with a voice. Sonia is the part of MySphere that listens.

  • Speaks their language

    A grandmother who's lived here thirty years may still think in Spanish, Hindi, or Mandarin. Sonia meets her there, and that preference carries straight into matching.

  • Hears what forms miss

    “I like walking, but my knee is bad on hills.” “My husband passed last spring.” The details that make or break a match come up naturally when someone is talking.

  • Hands off to a human

    Sonia isn't the companion. She's the intake. The goal of every conversation is a real person showing up at the door.

  • Keeps family in the loop

    The adult kid three time zones away gets to know someone is stopping by, without having to coordinate every visit themselves.
